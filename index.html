05 — The Idea

LUGGAGE IS PART OF THE JOURNEY.

A suitcase isn't usually the reason someone travels. It's what comes with them.

It gets thrown into the trunk, rolled through unfamiliar streets, carried up hotel stairs, taken through airports, and eventually brought home with everything collected along the way.

That makes luggage uniquely suited to storytelling.

Rather than creating traditional product advertising, I want to create films where the product naturally belongs within the story.

The journey comes first.
The product becomes part of it.

07 — The Approach

PRODUCT, WITHOUT THE PRODUCT-AD FEEL.

I don't want the luggage to simply sit in front of a camera.

I want it to move.
To travel.
To be used.
To become part of the environment and the story.

Every frame would be designed to showcase the product naturally — from the details and materials to the way it moves through real environments.

The result is content that feels authentic to the world of travel while still giving the product the attention it deserves.

08 — Why It Works

Travel content should make people want to go somewhere.

Authentic Fit

Luggage naturally belongs inside the real moments of travel: airports, roads, hotels, cities, and everything in between.

Premium Positioning

Reusable Content

Instead of one short-lived post, the campaign creates a library of assets that can live across social, ads, email, websites, and future campaigns.

The product gets them there.
The story makes them want to go.
